Distance and displacement

Distance is the total length of a path (scalar). Displacement is the change in position in a specified direction (vector).

Speed and velocity

Average speed = total distance ÷ total time. Average velocity = displacement ÷ time. SI unit: metres per second (m/s).

Acceleration

a = (v − u) / t, where u is initial velocity, v is final velocity, and t is elapsed time. SI unit: m/s².

Worked example

A cyclist increases velocity from 4 m/s to 12 m/s in 4 s. Acceleration = (12 − 4) / 4 = 2 m/s².

Try it

A vehicle changes velocity from 5 m/s to 17 m/s in 6 s. Calculate its average acceleration.

Show answer

(17 − 5) / 6 = 2 m/s².
