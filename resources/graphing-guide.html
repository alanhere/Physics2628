Graphing Experimental Data

Sample skills guide.
  1. Place the independent variable on the horizontal axis and dependent variable on the vertical axis, unless instructed otherwise.
  2. Label each axis with the quantity and unit.
  3. Choose even scales that use most of the graph area.
  4. Plot measurements accurately and draw an appropriate best-fit line or curve.
  5. For a straight line, choose two widely separated points on the line to calculate slope = change in y ÷ change in x.
  6. State the slope's units and interpret its physical meaning.
