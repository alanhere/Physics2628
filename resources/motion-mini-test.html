Motion Mini-Test

Original sample practice test, not a previous school assessment.
  1. Define average speed. (2 marks)
  2. Explain the difference between distance and displacement. (3 marks)
  3. A runner travels 150 m in 30 s. Calculate average speed. (3 marks)
  4. A car accelerates from 3 m/s to 15 m/s in 4 s. Calculate average acceleration. (3 marks)
  5. Explain what the slope of a distance–time graph represents. (2 marks)
Show indicative answers
  1. Total distance divided by total time.
  2. Distance is scalar path length; displacement is vector change in position.
  3. 150/30 = 5 m/s.
  4. (15 − 3)/4 = 3 m/s².
  5. Speed for a straight-line graph with positive slope.
